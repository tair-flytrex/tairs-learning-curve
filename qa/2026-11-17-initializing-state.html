What is the initializing state (emc and GCS) and what other states are there for this type?

MissionsServicesMessaging

What INITIALIZING is

It's the first value of the mission computer state that emc reports to the GCS: "initializing, not yet ready to receive a mission". emc sends the state to Mission Manager as an onboard_state message ({"state_type": "initializing", "until", "failure_mode", "problems"}). The GCS uses it to know what the drone is doing and whether it can accept a mission.

The same list of states is defined in three places:

  • app/.../server/messages/uplink/MissionComputerState.kt: StateType.INITIALIZING("initializing"). What emc sends to the GCS (JSON string value).
  • services/drone_facade/internal_sdk/protos/drone_facade.proto: MISSION_COMPUTER_STATE_INITIALIZING = 1. What drone_facade sends to emc in UpdateMissionComputerState on the fcm exchange.
  • system_tests/system_tests/mm_schemas.py: MissionComputerStateType.INITIALIZING. The Mission Manager puppet's copy, for tests.

When the drone is INITIALIZING

  • At emc startup. MissionComputer.triggerSettingsUpdate() reports INITIALIZING, then imports settings from Oversight and retries until that works.
  • After a mission ends or fails fatally. emc tears down its devices and mission logic and runs the same settings update, so the drone is INITIALIZING again until it's ready.
  • While setup keeps failing. If devices or mission logic fail to come up (initDevicesAndLogic), emc retries after a delay. Meanwhile the drone stays INITIALIZING and droneError holds the reason.
  • When the aircraft is out of service after a failed OTA update. drone_facade sends MISSION_COMPUTER_STATE_OUT_OF_SERVICE. The GCS has no matching state yet, so emc sets SystemState.outOfService, reports the error "Out of service", and maps the state to INITIALIZING (there's a TODO in NewMissionLogic.java for a dedicated state).

It ends when the mission logic starts: NewMissionLogic.start() moves straight to IdleState, and the drone reports IDLE. Inside emc's own state machine, InitialState is just a placeholder named INITIALIZING that exists until the logic starts.

All the states of this type (33 for the GCS; the facade proto also has UNSPECIFIED and OUT_OF_SERVICE):

  • On the ground: initializing (starting up, not ready for a mission), idle (waiting for a mission), preflight (has a mission, before takeoff), postflight (mission finished, post-flight tasks).
  • Takeoff and flight: taking_off, inflight_to_dest (flying to the delivery point), inflight_to_pickup, inflight_to_dock, inflight_home, landing_home.
  • Delivery (wire drop): waiting_wiredrop (waiting for confirmation to lower the package), performing_wiredrop.
  • Pickup: preparing_for_pickup (lowering the hook), waiting_for_package_loaded_confirmation, lifting_package, wait_for_door_closure.
  • Dock: waiting_on_dock, taking_off_from_dock, landing_on_dock, picking_up_from_dock.
  • Safe-up: safing_up, safing_up_to_land, safed_up (hovering at a safe altitude at the nearest semaphore).
  • Abort and emergency: abort_home, abort_home_landing, emergency_landing.
  • DAA (Detect And Avoid): descending (in the DAA descent), descended (descent finished, hovering).
  • Off-nominal: rc_in_control (the pilot took over with the remote control), executing_fallback_mission (the FC took over), fc_loss (lost the link to the FC), parachute_triggered.
  • Facade proto only, never sent to the GCS: UNSPECIFIED (protobuf default), OUT_OF_SERVICE (reported to the GCS as initializing).

Since the move to drone_facade, the facade decides most of these states and emc only passes them on. INITIALIZING is the main one emc still sets by itself (the first three cases above).
