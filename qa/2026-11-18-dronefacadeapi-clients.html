Who accesses DroneFacadeAPI?

MessagingServices

Only emc calls DroneFacadeAPI. Its one client is DroneFacadeCommunicator.java. No Python service, test or puppet opens a stub to port 50051. The other hits in the repo are the server itself (drone_facade/main.py, grpc_services/drone_facade_api_service.py) and the BlackBox log viewer, which only parses log text.

What emc calls, and from where

  • initialize (10 s deadline): emc startup; sends runtime config to the facade.
  • report_server_message: MissionServer forwards every ground message (wired up at MissionComputer.java:878).
  • heartbeat (client stream): NewMissionLogic.java:416.
  • trigger_non_navigation_state: NewMissionLogic (several places).
  • trigger_inflight_failure_mode: NewMissionLogic (many places).
  • report_takeoff_prohibition_reasons: NewMissionLogic (around lines 3064–3340).
  • custom_command and get_altimeter_measurement (500 ms deadline): defined in DroneFacadeCommunicator.java; callers not traced.

The other direction

Traffic back to emc doesn't use gRPC. The facade answers asynchronously on the fcm exchange, with topics such as FacadeResponseToServer. The facade is also a gRPC client of fcm (FcmAPI) and software_updater (SoftwareUpdaterAPI), but nothing calls into it except emc.
